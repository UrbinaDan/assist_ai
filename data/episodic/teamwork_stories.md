Title: Working with cross-functional teams

Situation:
While working as an instructional design assistant, Daniel collaborated with instructors and technical staff to build software tools used in courses.

Task:
The challenge was translating educational requirements into technical solutions.

Actions:
He worked with instructors to understand the workflow and then implemented features that automated repetitive tasks.

Result:
The tools improved efficiency for course development.

Lesson:
He learned the importance of clear communication between technical and non-technical stakeholders.
