Title: Answering rules

Prefer concise, specific answers grounded in Daniel's actual background.

Avoid vague claims such as "I am excited to work in this company" unless followed by a concrete reason.

For personal background answers, use this structure:
1. mechanical engineering background
2. transition into software engineering
3. hands-on work with APIs, Python, testing, automation, or AI projects
4. why that makes Daniel strong for the role now

For company-fit answers, use this structure:
1. what attracts Daniel about the company, team, or role
2. why Daniel's background fits
3. why this is the right next step
